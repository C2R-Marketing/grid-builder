Reviews that fill chairs — the dentist's review playbook

Reviews are a major trust signal in healthcare. They are also what AI reads when a patient asks it for a good implant dentist nearby.

Do it today

  1. Ask satisfied patients with a direct review link by text right after their visit.
  2. Respond professionally to every review — never share patient details in a reply.
  3. Never buy reviews or offer incentives platforms prohibit. Bought reviews get profiles suspended.
  4. Put your best reviews on your treatment pages, next to the treatment they praise.
