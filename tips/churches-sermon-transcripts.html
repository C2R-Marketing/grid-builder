Sermon transcripts AI can actually read

Video is not searchable. A decade of recorded sermons with no transcripts is a decade of content neither Google nor any AI engine can read. Text is the fix.

Do it today

  1. Pick your ten best sermons and publish a transcript for each — plain text, headings, no PDF-only.
  2. Put the sermon's one-sentence answer in the first paragraph. AI quotes first paragraphs.
  3. Tag each transcript with its topic: baptism, grief, kids ministry, giving — the questions people actually ask.
  4. Link every transcript to your Plan a Visit page so a reader can take the next step.
  5. Update your most important pages at least every 60 days. Fresh text gets cited; stale text gets skipped.
