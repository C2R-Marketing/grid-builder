Google Business Profile for dentists — the 10-minute fix list

Most new patients search “dentist near me” and book one of the first clinics they see. Your profile decides whether that is you.

Do it today

  1. Claim and verify the profile. Choose the right category, add clinic photos, hours, and phone.
  2. Make your name, address, and phone identical on Yelp, Zocdoc, Healthgrades, and your site.
  3. Add a photo of the office, the team, and the treatment rooms. Real photos beat stock.
  4. Ask happy patients for a Google review after treatment — and reply to every review.
  5. Post weekly: hours changes, new treatments, team news. An active profile ranks.
